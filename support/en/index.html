Reframiq · Help

Compare two photos simply.

Choose photos

Select photo A and photo B through Android's system picker. Reframiq does not need access to your entire photo library.

Switch views

Use the slider for a direct before-and-after wipe, compare both images side by side, or adjust the overlay opacity.

Share or save

Share opens Android's share menu. Save asks you to choose a destination. Your original images are not changed.

Privacy

Images are processed locally and temporarily for the comparison. Reframiq Free has no camera capture, project timeline, or automatic alignment. Read the privacy policy.

Reframiq Pro

The separate Reframiq Pro app supports repeat captures and longer-term photo documentation. Read the Pro privacy notice.
