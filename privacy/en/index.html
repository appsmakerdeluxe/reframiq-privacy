Reframiq · Privacy

Your photos stay with you.

Last updated: November 6, 2026 · Applies from Reframiq 2.0.2

1. Controller and contact

AppsMakerDeluxe
appsmakerdeluxe@gmail.com

2. Data processed

Reframiq Photo Compare processes two existing images that you choose through Android's system picker. The app temporarily copies them to its private cache for display and comparison. It does not create projects, profiles, capture histories, or accounts.

3. On-device image processing

The slider, side-by-side view, and transparent overlay are rendered directly on your device. The app does not automatically align images or capture photos with the camera. Original files outside the app are not modified. Images and comparison data are not transferred to AppsMakerDeluxe or other recipients.

4. Temporary storage, export, and sharing

Selected images are copied to the private app cache only for the current comparison. If you save or share a comparison, the generated file is passed to the location or app you choose. Exported files may remain outside Reframiq.

5. Retention and deletion

Temporary comparison files are cleaned regularly; the app removes expired cache files the next time it starts. Android removes private app storage when you uninstall the app. Files you exported must be deleted at their chosen destination.

6. Permissions and security

Reframiq requires no runtime permission. Images are opened only through Android's system picker. The app has no camera or internet permission and uses no location, contacts, broad media, or storage permission.

7. No accounts, ads, or tracking

Reframiq uses no accounts, cloud sync, advertising, in-app purchases, analytics, telemetry, or tracking SDKs and does not sell or share personal data.

8. Children and audience

Reframiq is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not designed specifically for children. The app does not collect data through AppsMakerDeluxe servers.

Reframiq Pro

Reframiq Pro is a separate app for repeat captures and longer-term photo documentation. It has a separate Reframiq Pro privacy notice.

9. This website

This static site is delivered through GitHub Pages. AppsMakerDeluxe uses no cookies, trackers, analytics, or advertising scripts. GitHub may process technical request information such as IP addresses and server logs under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

10. Changes and contact

This notice will be updated before data processing or features change materially. Contact appsmakerdeluxe@gmail.com with questions.
