Privacy for Reframiq Pro

AppGallery preparation · Updated October 25, 2026

Reframiq Pro has not been released. The app is being prepared for paid distribution through HUAWEI AppGallery and has not been released. There is currently no download and no sale. This notice describes the locally verified app and will be checked again against the final store build before release.

1. Controller and contact

AppsMakerDeluxe
appsmakerdeluxe@gmail.com

2. Data on your device

The app processes selected photos, projects, capture profiles, annotations, comparisons, PDF reports, time-lapse videos and backups locally on the device. Projects and settings remain in private app storage. Import and export use Android system pickers; a chosen storage location or app receives access only when you save or share a file. There is no account, cloud, analytics, advertising, telemetry, or transfer to AppsMakerDeluxe.

3. Permissions

The app requests only camera access at runtime, used for captures you explicitly start. Photo selection and saving use Android system pickers. The app does not use internet, location, microphone, or notification permissions, advertising, or telemetry.

4. Exports and original metadata

PDF, image and video exports omit GPS and device EXIF data. PDF reports show the project name and capture date. Comparison JPEGs omit date and note text by default; markups you add appear in the export. Project archives and full backups may contain unchanged original photos with embedded metadata. The destination or app you choose receives access to files you save or share.

5. Encrypted full backup

Full backups can be created and restored locally with password-based authenticated encryption. The password is neither stored nor transmitted. Backup files saved or shared outside the app are retained independently of Reframiq.

6. Purchase

HUAWEI AppGallery is planned for paid distribution. Huawei handles the purchase and payment processing; the app does not process payment details. The app is not yet available in the store and no sale has started.

7. Retention and deletion

You can delete projects and captures in the app. Android removes private app storage on uninstall. You must delete exported files and backups at their chosen storage locations.

8. This website

This static site uses no cookies, trackers, analytics, or advertising scripts of its own. GitHub Pages may process technical request data. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

9. Changes and contact

Before release, this notice will be checked again against the final store feature set. Contact appsmakerdeluxe@gmail.com.
